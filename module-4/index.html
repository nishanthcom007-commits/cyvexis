SECTION 01 // VECTORS 📱

The Multi-Channel Threat Landscape

Overview: Threat actors routinely bypass corporate email gateways by shifting attack vectors to SMS (Smishing), direct mobile messaging (WhatsApp/Telegram), and targeted voice calls. Mobile devices naturally conceal risks due to smaller viewports, absent hover states, and truncated domain names.

Attack Mechanics: Common lures weaponize psychological urgency, including:

  • Failed courier package deliveries with urgent "customs clearance fees"
  • Fake financial fraud warnings ("Unauthorized wire attempt: click to freeze")
  • Shortened and obfuscated redirects (e.g., bit.ly, tinyurl, lookalike domains)
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Never click inbound links in SMS or messaging apps. Disregard instructions to reply "STOP". Access enterprise and delivery services solely via verified browser bookmarks or official standalone mobile applications.

SECTION 02 // VISUAL EXPLOITS 🔳

Quishing (QR Code Phishing)

Overview: Quick Response (QR) code phishing embeds malicious redirection matrixes into physical printouts, parking meters, conference venue signage, counterfeit office MFA update flyers, or as graphical email attachments.

Attack Mechanics: Traditional text-based email security gateways often fail to parse URLs embedded inside images. Scanning the code moves the transaction off monitored enterprise workstations and directly onto personal mobile browsers that lack corporate EDR monitoring.

  • Physical sticker overlays covertly pasted over legitimate payment scanners
  • Embedded QR codes in emails requesting "Scan to re-authenticate Microsoft 365"
  • Obfuscated redirect chains routing to credential harvesting portals
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Inspect physical QR codes for physical tampering or adhesive sticker overlays. Enable link preview within your camera app and rigorously inspect the Fully Qualified Domain Name (FQDN) before visiting.

SECTION 03 // SYNTHETIC DEEPFAKES 🎙️

Vishing & Real-Time AI Voice Cloning

Overview: Voice Phishing (Vishing) has evolved with generative neural audio synthesis. Threat actors can now generate convincing voice deepfakes of company executives, managers, or vendors using as little as 3 to 5 seconds of public audio harvested from webinars or earnings calls.

Auditory & Operational Indicators: Real-time synthetic speech synthesis exhibits distinct artifacts:

  • Abrupt latency delays when interrupted or asked off-script questions
  • Unnatural prosody, robotic inflections, and strange pitch transitions
  • Audio clipping, phase distortion, and sterile acoustic backgrounds
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Mandatory Out-of-Band (OOB) Verification: Never trust inbound Caller ID (telephony spoofing is trivial). Immediately hang up and dial the individual back using the official corporate internal directory.

SECTION 04 // ENTERPRISE SOP 📋

Verification Protocols & Incident SOP

Overview: Technical defenses must be backed by institutional governance that prevents social engineering from succeeding even when a perimeter deception lure breaches the organization.

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP):

  • Mandatory Callback Verification: Required for any wire transfer, payroll account update, or MFA hardware token reset.
  • Strict Dual-Authorization: At least two authorized stakeholders must independently validate financial disbursements.
  • Forensic Incident Reporting: Capture uncropped screenshots, document sending phone numbers, preserve email headers, and escalate to SecOps/SOC immediately.
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Zero-Shaming Culture: If you accidentally scan an untrusted code or approve an authentication prompt, notify SecOps immediately. Fast reporting allows defenders to revoke session tokens in seconds.
