SECTION 01 // TELEMETRY 🕵️

Recognizing Signs of Account Compromise

Overview: Cyber breaches rarely announce themselves with blaring ransomware notes immediately; they begin quietly. Detecting subtle, early anomalies halts lateral movement and limits operational blast radius before privilege escalation occurs.

Key Compromise Indicators:

  • Unauthorized Inbox Rules: Stealth forwarding rules that divert invoices, billing updates, or security notifications directly into obscure folders (e.g., RSS Feeds, Archive, or Trash).
  • Anomaly Logins: Concurrent authentications from distant geographical regions within physically impossible timeframes ("Impossible Travel"), or unexpected user-agent browser strings.
  • Security Configuration Tampering: Unsolicited registration of secondary MFA hardware keys, authenticator apps, or altered recovery email addresses.
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Treat any unexpected inbox rule or unfamiliar MFA device as an active security breach. Immediately alert SecOps rather than simply deleting the rule.

SECTION 02 // CONTAINMENT ⏱️

The "First 60 Minutes" Emergency Triage SOP

Overview: A structured, ordered response halts the attacker's persistent session cookies and prevents secondary access attempts.

Immediate 4-Step Triage Execution:

  • Immediate Step 1 — Session Invalidation: Terminate all active browser sessions, revoke OAuth application authorizations, and invalidate active refresh tokens across cloud identity providers (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Okta).
  • Immediate Step 2 — Credential Reset: Update primary passwords only AFTER terminating external sessions and removing unauthorized MFA devices.
  • Immediate Step 3 — Mailbox & Delegation Audit: Inspect inbox forwarding rules, delegator access lists, transport rules, and automatic reply filters.
  • Immediate Step 4 — Endpoint Isolation: Disconnect suspected physical or virtual endpoints from Wi-Fi and Ethernet without powering down the machine to preserve volatile RAM forensics.
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Never power off a compromised endpoint. Powering down permanently destroys volatile memory (RAM) where running malware processes, unencrypted keys, and active network connections reside.

SECTION 03 // FRAUD PREVENTION 💼

Mitigating Business Email Compromise (BEC)

Overview: Business Email Compromise (BEC) syndicates rarely deploy malware right away. Instead, they dwell silently in executive or accounting mailboxes, monitoring invoice threads until payment details are ready to exchange.

Attack Playbook & Interception:

  • Adversaries inject altered bank account details via PDF invoices right before wire execution.
  • They create mail rules to delete legitimate payment inquiries from real vendors.
  • They fabricate internal "executive approvals" citing time-sensitive confidential audits.
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Mandatory Out-of-Band Callback Verification: Any update to wire routing coordinates or beneficiary accounts requires verbal phone verification using pre-vetted numbers from internal master records—never numbers listed on the new invoice or email.

SECTION 04 // FORENSICS & COMMS 🔒

Evidence Preservation & Safe Reporting

Overview: Proper incident coordination ensures evidence remains legally defensible while preventing attackers from discovering that defenders have begun remediation.

Operational Reporting Protocols:

  • Log Integrity: Export sign-in logs, cloud audit logs, and raw RFC 822 email message headers prior to modifying compromised infrastructure.
  • Out-of-Band Emergency Channels: Never coordinate response activities via the suspected compromised email system (the attacker may be actively reading your emails). Use dedicated out-of-band bridges or encrypted channels.
  • Internal vs External Communication: Escalate strictly to the designated Incident Response Lead and SecOps triage team following Cyvexis breach reporting guidelines.
🛡️ Key Defense Protocol

Avoid Tipping Off Adversaries: Coordinating response within compromised email threads prompts attackers to deploy ransomware or purge forensic traces immediately.

First 60 Minutes Triage Checklist

Execute these ordered response stages to contain account compromise before privilege escalation:

STAGE 01
Session Invalidation
Revoke all active browser sessions, OAuth permissions, and refresh tokens across cloud identity providers.
STAGE 02
Credential Reset
Purge unauthorized MFA devices, then reset master credentials to lock out the adversary.
STAGE 03
Mailbox Audit
Examine hidden inbox forwarding rules, delegator access permissions, and automated transport rules.
STAGE 04
Network Isolation
Unplug Ethernet & disconnect Wi-Fi. Do not power down the workstation to preserve volatile RAM.

Out-of-Band (OOB) Incident Communications

When an account or tenant is compromised, never coordinate incident response over internal email.

⚠️ CRITICAL OPERATIONAL WARNING:

If the adversary possesses global tenant observation privileges or has compromised an executive mailbox, discussing containment in that channel tips them off, allowing them to delete event logs or accelerate ransomware deployment.
