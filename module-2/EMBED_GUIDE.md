## 🎯 Pedagogical Architecture

1. **Understand**:
   - **Credential Stuffing** (ATT&CK T1110.004): Automated mass credential replay from external breach dumps.
   - **Password Spraying** (ATT&CK T1110.003): Low-and-slow single password sweeps evading lockout thresholds.
   - **MFA Fatigue / Prompt Bombing** (ATT&CK T1621): Psychological inundation, sleep disruption, and cognitive exhaustion.
   - **Strict Zero User-Shaming Philosophy**: Emphasizes that falling victim to prompt bombing is the result of intentional attacker manipulation and sensory overload, not stupidity.

2. **Recognize (Live Mock Simulation)**:
   - Realistic mobile smartphone simulation at 2:14 AM.
   - Incoming push barrage arriving every 4.2 seconds with simulated vibration and haptic audio.
   - Interactive options for `[Deny / Block]` and `[Approve (Tap to test)]`.
   - Explores the 3 psychological triggers: circadian vulnerability, sensory desensitization, and authority spoofing.

3. **Decide (The Defense Hierarchy)**:
   - Password Managers (Bitwarden, 1Password, Apple Keychain) for zero-reuse.
   - Passphrases > Complex short strings (Entropy comparison lab).
   - MFA Defense Hierarchy: S-Tier (FIDO2 Passkeys/YubiKeys), A-Tier (Number Matching push), D-Tier (SMS/Voice).

4. **Respond (Emergency Containment Protocol)**:
   - Step 1: Change password immediately from a clean device.
   - Step 2: Terminate all active sessions & revoke OAuth tokens ("Sign out of all devices").
   - Step 3: Promptly alert IT Admin / SOC team with zero fear of reprimand.

5. **Practice (3-Scenario Interactive Quiz)**:
   - Realistic workplace dilemmas with instant constructive explanations and score tracking.

6. **Remember**:
   - Interactive takeaway checklist for daily identity hygiene.

7. **Mandatory Security Callout Card**:
   - High-prominence permanent warning banner at the footer:
     > **"Official Cyvexis Safety Notice:** Cyvexis will NEVER ask for your passwords, email credentials, MFA codes, or sensitive personal information. If anyone asks for these under our name, do not engage. Report to cyvexis.cybersecurity@gmail.com."
